Home gym, basement. This is the canonical equipment inventory; exercise

## What's available

- **Power rack** with multi-grip **pull-up bar** (wide / neutral / close).
- **Plate-loaded cable tower** built into the rack:
  - **ONE stack / one cable** — high pulley (pulldowns) and a low position
    (rows) share the same plate-loaded carriage. Only **one cable exercise can
    be loaded at a time**.
  - Attachments: **D-handle** (single), short **row bar** (~1 ft), long
    **pulldown bar** (bent ends), **rope**.
  - The rope unlocks face pulls (rear delt), rope pushdowns, rope overhead
    triceps ext, rope curls.
- **Dip handles** mounted on the rack (see "dips" constraint below).
- **Barbell + bumper plates** (red/yellow/black) plus small change plates for
  micro-loading.
- **SelectTech 1090 adjustable dumbbells** — **90 lb/hand max**, 5 lb
  increments. Enough to anchor DB presses, rows, RDLs, split squats long-term.
- **Adjustable bench** (~flat / 30 / 45 / 70). The "leg peg" rollers are
  **bracing only — NOT a working leg developer** (no leg-extension / leg-curl
  resistance).
- **Ab wheel.**
- **Push-up handles** (elevated deficit push-ups for extra stretch).
- **Yoga mats.**

## Hard constraints (do not violate in programming)

- **Superset equipment rule** — one pair of SelectTech DBs + one cable stack means
  **within a superset the two moves must use different equipment.** No **cable+cable**
  (one stack, re-pin every alternation), no **DB+DB** (only one pair; re-dialing the
  SelectTechs every alternation — the sole exception is two DB moves at the *identical*
  weight), no **barbell+barbell** (re-loading plates every alternation). Legal pairings:
  DB+cable, DB+bodyweight, cable+bodyweight, barbell+anything-else. **Separate straight
  blocks** may reuse the same equipment at a different weight (finish, rest, re-load, go).
- **Hamstrings: barbell / DB hinge only** — RDL, deadlift, stiff-leg, good
  morning, single-leg DB RDL. **NO leg-curl machine, NO leg-extension, NO bands,
  NO GHR/Nordic anchor** (bench pegs are braces, not a leg developer).
- **No machines** other than the plate-loaded cable tower.
- **Elbow-safe bias** — avoid positions that reliably irritate the elbow.

## Notes affecting exercise selection

- **Dips:** station is available, but Ryan can currently do ~1 rep. Treat dips as
  a **bodyweight movement to build up** (chest-lean / forward torso for chest
  bias), **not a loadable anchor** yet. Revisit weighted dips once bodyweight
  reps climb.
- **Side-delt stretch without a 2nd cable:** side-lying DB lateral raise (braced
  on the incline bench) gives the stretch-at-bottom that standing DB raises lack.
- **Lat stretch without spending the cable:** DB pullover (dumbbell, loads the
  lat lengthened) frees the cable for pulldowns/rows.
- **SelectTech width vs. floor clearance:** the SelectTechs are wide, so any prone/
  face-down bench move where the DBs hang toward the floor at full stretch (e.g.
  chest-supported DB row) needs the body raised (foam block) or the DBs hit the floor —
  uncomfortable. **Avoid those setups.** Use **single-arm DB row** (bench-braced, DB
  hangs off the side into open space — clears the floor, no block) for horizontal pulling,
  and the **standing single-arm cable reverse fly** for rear delt (no bench, no block).
